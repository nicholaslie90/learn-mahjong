How scoring works

Faan to units

  • Won on a discard: the discarder pays the units alone.
  • Self-drawn: each of the other three pays the units.
  • A penalty (chombo): the offender pays the chosen faan, in units, to each of the other three.
  • Money: each unit is worth the rate you set.

Points for a win

  • Base points are fu × 2^(han + 2).
  • Limits: mangan 2,000 base (5 han, or a base over 2,000), haneman 3,000 (6–7 han), baiman 4,000 (8–10), sanbaiman 6,000 (11–12), yakuman 8,000 (13 han, or each yakuman).
  • Ron: the discarder pays 4× base, or 6× when the winner is the dealer.
  • Tsumo: the dealer pays 2× base and the others 1×; when the dealer wins, everyone pays 2×.
  • Every payment rounds up to the next 100.

Honba and riichi sticks

  • Each honba adds 300 to a ron, or 100 from each payer on a tsumo.
  • Riichi costs a 1,000 stick. The next winner takes every stick on the table; after a draw they carry over.
  • The dealer keeps the seat after winning or being tenpai at a draw, and a honba is added. A non-dealer win passes the deal and clears the honba.

Draws and the rest

  • Draw: the noten players pay 3,000 between them to the tenpai players. All or none tenpai: nobody pays.
  • Abortive draw (triple ron, nine terminals, four winds, four riichi, four kongs): nobody pays, the dealer repeats and a honba is added.
  • Double ron: both winners are paid; the one nearer the discarder's turn takes the honba and the sticks.
  • Pao: the player who fed the last set of big three dragons or big four winds pays that yakuman whole on a tsumo, or half of it on a ron.
  • Chombo: a reverse mangan. A dealer pays 4,000 to everyone; anyone else pays 4,000 to the dealer and 2,000 to the others. The hand is replayed.

Final score

  • Final = (points − return) ÷ 1,000 + uma for the placing.
  • First place also takes the oka — what the return score held back from everyone, (return − 25,000) × 4 — and any sticks left on the table.
  • Ties go to the seat nearer the first dealer.
  • Money: each point of final score is worth the rate you set.
